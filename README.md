# Mix It Up

![A watercolor fall banner titled Mix It Up, with red, yellow, and blue paint circles overlapping into orange, green, and purple on one side, glowing red, green, and blue light circles overlapping into yellow, cyan, and magenta on the other, and falling leaves, sweaters, hiking boots, a creek, and a mug of hot cocoa around them.](cover.png)

Mix It Up is a color-mixing game for one player, age eight and up.

## Play It

[https://jeremynyc4.github.io/game-01-mix-it-up/](https://jeremynyc4.github.io/game-01-mix-it-up/)

You need a phone or a computer with a web browser, and the game is for one player.

## How to Play

1. Choose Paint or Light. Paint starts from red, yellow, and blue. Light starts from red, green, and blue.
2. Tap Start.
3. Each round shows a first color and a goal color. Tap the color that mixes with the first color to make the goal.
4. If you pick wrong, the game shows what your mix makes. Tap another color to try again.
5. A round counts only if you get it right on your first tap. Get two of the three rounds right to win a digital cup of hot cocoa.
6. Tap Play Again to start a new game.
